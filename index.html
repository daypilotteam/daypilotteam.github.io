DayPilot questions

Is the DayPilot app free on Android?

+

Yes. DayPilot: Habit & Day Planner is a free Android app on Google Play, with no sign-up and no login.

Does DayPilot require an account or sign-up?

+

No. Open the app, pick what you want to improve and start. There's no sign-up and no login.

Where is my data stored?

+

On your phone. Your habits, diary, goals, bills and spending entries are saved on your device, not on our servers.

Is DayPilot an offline habit tracker?

+

Yes. This version of DayPilot works completely offline: the habit tracker, daily planner, diary, bill reminders and expense tracker all work without internet, and your entries stay in local storage on your phone.

Can I track monthly bills and expenses in DayPilot?

+

Yes. Bill Reminder tracks rent, EMI, electricity, internet, insurance and any other bill, reminds you before the due date and lets you mark it paid in one tap. Monthly bills repeat on their own. Spending works as an offline expense tracker: log what you spend by category and see each month at a glance.

Can I track water intake and meals?

+

Yes. The water intake tracker fills a ring on Home toward your daily goal (for example 2.5 L), 250 ml at a time, even straight from a notification. The meal log covers breakfast, lunch and dinner, with quick picks from foods you logged before, and the Food Tracker shows your meals day by day.

Can I create my own routine?

+

Yes. DayPilot builds a starting routine from what you want to improve, and you can change it before you begin. Add your own habits to any part of the day (Morning routine, Day, Evening, Night routine or a meal) as a simple check-off, an amount like 2 litres, time in minutes, or a meal log. Remove anything you don’t need.

Can I edit my routine after I’ve created it?

+

Yes, any time, in Daily Routine. Switch habits on or off, change targets, drag to reorder, or add new ones. When you save, choose whether the changes also apply to today, and to past days. Turning a habit off never deletes its history. Try it above ↑

Can I add an activity for a specific day?

+

Habits in your routine repeat every day. For something on a particular day, like a doctor’s visit, a bill or a birthday, add a Reminder with that date and time. It can also repeat weekly, monthly or yearly. If a habit isn’t needed on a certain day, you can skip it for that day.

What can I track?

+

Your daily routine and habits, water and meals, goals and targets with milestones, Bill Reminder, monthly spending, a private diary with moods and #tags, your own reminders, daily nudges, and achievements and streaks.
